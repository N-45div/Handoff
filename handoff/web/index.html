Labs don’t buy dumps. They buy tasks.

Handoff is the agent that turns a swarm or coding-agent transcript into a task: instruction, episode, and a verifier. The verifier is whether promises were kept, dropped, duplicated, or never claimed — and that is the reward.

Open the desk Build from a coding log

01

Drop a transcript

JSONL or CSV. Speaker + content, or Cursor / Claude-shaped role blocks. Extra keys ignored. Secrets never become a task.

02

Score the promises

Who promised what to whom. Kept, dropped, unclaimed, duplicate — cited from the log via Zetaris. That is why the swarm stalled.

03

Emit the task

Instruction + OpenEnv episode + handoff-reward@0.1. Seller sets the list price. Handoff takes 20%. Village data is never listed.

Who it’s for

  • Labs — consented tasks with a verifier, not scraped chat.
  • Teams with traces — list a task, set the price, keep 80%.
  • Researchers — investigate why a swarm stalled. Village is research-only.

Raw logs are worth little. The only way to make them worth anything is turning them into tasks agents can learn from.

That is the product. Not a marketplace of JSONL. Not Alibi (prior work: past-claims audit).
